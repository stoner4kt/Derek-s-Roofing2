Roof waterproofing that addresses the source of leaks

Derek's Roofing provides roof waterproofing for flat roofs, IBR sheeting and tiled roofs across Cape Town. We inspect the roof and identify failed seals, membranes, coatings and penetrations before recommending the appropriate repair.

Depending on the roof, our waterproofing work can include:

  • Bitumen membrane systems
  • Liquid-applied waterproofing
  • Roof coatings and protective paint
  • IBR sheeting sealing
  • Penetration and flashing sealing
  • Leak-source inspection and drainage checks
