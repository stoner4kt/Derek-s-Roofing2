Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most often.

Which areas do you service?
We're based in Welgemoed and service Cape Town and the surrounding Northern Suburbs, including Bellville, Durbanville and Tyger Valley. Contact us to confirm coverage for your area.
How quickly can you give me a quote?
We aim to respond to enquiries within one business day, and can usually schedule an on-site inspection within the week.
Do you offer emergency leak repairs?
Yes — call or WhatsApp us directly for urgent leaks and we'll do our best to assist as quickly as possible.
Do you provide a guarantee on your work?
All workmanship is carried out to industry standard, and any issues with completed work should be reported to us promptly so we can assess and resolve them.
What roofing materials do you work with?
We work with tiled, IBR sheeting, and shingle roofing, as well as flat-roof waterproofing systems, timber decking and pergola structures.
Do I need to be home for the quote?
It helps for someone to be available to unlock access and answer questions, but it isn't always essential — let us know what works for you.

Still have a question?

Get in touch and we'll get back to you directly.
