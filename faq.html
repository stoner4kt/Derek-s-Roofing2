Roofing Questions Cape Town — FAQs

Answers to the questions we hear most often about roof repairs, re-roofing, gutter cleaning, waterproofing and more.

Which areas do you service?
We service the Western Cape, with our base in Welgemoed and primary focus on Cape Town and the Northern Suburbs (including Bellville, Durbanville and Tyger Valley). A call-out fee applies for areas outside the Northern suburbs of Cape Town — the price depends on the area. Contact us to confirm coverage and any call-out fee for your location.
How quickly can you give me a quote?
We aim to respond to enquiries within one business day, and can usually schedule an on-site inspection within the week.
Do you offer emergency leak repairs?
Yes — call or WhatsApp us directly for urgent leaks and we'll do our best to assist as quickly as possible.
Do you provide a guarantee on your work?
All workmanship is carried out to industry standard, and any issues with completed work should be reported to us promptly so we can assess and resolve them.
What roofing materials do you work with?
We work with tiled, IBR sheeting, and shingle roofing, as well as flat-roof waterproofing systems, timber decking and pergola structures.
Do I need to be home for the quote?
It helps for someone to be available to unlock access and answer questions, but it isn't always essential — let us know what works for you.
How much does a roof repair cost in Cape Town?
Roof repair costs vary depending on the extent of the damage, materials involved and access to the roof. Minor repairs (replacing a few tiles or sealing a small flat roof leak) are significantly cheaper than larger structural repairs. We provide free, no-obligation quotes — contact us with a description or photos of the problem for an accurate estimate.
Do you do flat roof repairs and waterproofing?
Yes — flat roof leak repairs and waterproofing are one of our core services. We diagnose the source of the leak and apply the correct waterproofing solution, whether that's bitumen membrane, liquid-applied waterproofing or re-pitching the roof to improve drainage.
Do you offer gutter cleaning services?
Yes, we offer gutter cleaning, repair and full replacement. Blocked gutters are one of the leading causes of roof and fascia damage in Cape Town — we recommend an annual inspection and clean, especially after the wet winter season.
What is the difference between re-roofing and a roof replacement?
Re-roofing typically means laying a new layer of material over the existing roof (where structurally sound). A full roof replacement involves stripping the old material, inspecting and repairing the structure underneath, and installing a completely new roof. We'll advise which option is best for your home during the site inspection.
